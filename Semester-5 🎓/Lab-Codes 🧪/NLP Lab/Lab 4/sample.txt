Natural Language Processing is a fascinating field of Artificial Intelligence that helps computers understand, interpret, and generate human language. Companies are using NLP in chatbots, search engines, translation tools, and voice assistants every day! Before a machine can learn from text, the text must be cleaned and prepared properly. This step is called preprocessing, and it includes tokenization, lowercasing, removing punctuation, deleting stopwords, stemming, and lemmatization. Students who are learning these techniques often find that clean data produces better results than messy data. For example, running, runs, and ran all carry a similar meaning, but a computer treats them as different words unless we normalize them. Good preprocessing saves time, reduces noise, and improves the accuracy of every model that is trained afterwards.
